# 2.1.1. Impact of the Software Development Lifecycle on Testing

## Summary (ours)

The SDLC drives the scope and timing of activities, documentation depth, techniques, automation and the tester's role. In sequential models dynamic testing comes late; iterative ones test every iteration with heavy regression; agile favours light documentation, automation and experience-based techniques.
