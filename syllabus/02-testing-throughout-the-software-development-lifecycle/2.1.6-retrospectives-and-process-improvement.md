# 2.1.6. Retrospectives and Process Improvement

## Summary (ours)

Meetings at the end of iterations, milestones or projects discuss what to keep, what to improve and how. Outcomes go into the completion report. Benefits: effectiveness, better testware and test basis, team learning and closer dev-test cooperation.
