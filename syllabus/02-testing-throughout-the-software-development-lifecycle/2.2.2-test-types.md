## Summary (ours)

Functional covers "what" the system does; non-functional covers "how well", following the ISO/IEC 25010 characteristics (performance, compatibility, usability, reliability, security, maintainability, portability, safety). Black-box derives tests from the specification and white-box from the internal structure. All four apply at every level.
