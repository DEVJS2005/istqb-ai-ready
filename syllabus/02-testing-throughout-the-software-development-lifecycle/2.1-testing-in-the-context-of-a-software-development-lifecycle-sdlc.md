## Summary (ours)

An SDLC model describes how development phases and activities relate. Examples: waterfall and V-model (sequential), spiral and prototyping (iterative), Unified Process (incremental), plus practices such as Scrum, Kanban, XP, TDD, ATDD and BDD.
