## Summary (ours)

Maintenance may be corrective, adaptive or for improvement, planned or urgent (hot fix). Its scope depends on the risk, the size of the system and the size of the change. Triggers are modifications, upgrades or migrations of the environment (including data conversion) and retirement (data archiving, restore and retrieval).
