# 5.2.2. Project Risks and Product Risks

> **Shell.** This file does not contain the syllabus text. The summary is ours (not ISTQB text).

## Summary (ours)

Project risks come from management (organisation, people, technical, suppliers) and affect schedule, cost and scope. Product risks relate to quality characteristics (ISO 25010) and can cause dissatisfaction, losses, harm to third parties, penalties and even physical harm.
