# 5.1.4. Estimation Techniques

## Summary (ours)

Ratios and extrapolation are metrics-based (historical or current project data); Wideband Delphi (and Planning Poker) and three-point are expert-based. In three-point, E = (a + 4m + b) / 6 and SD = (b − a) / 6. Every estimate has assumptions and error, and large tasks should be broken down first.
