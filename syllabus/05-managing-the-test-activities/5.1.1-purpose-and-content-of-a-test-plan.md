# 5.1.1. Purpose and Content of a Test Plan

> **Shell.** This file does not contain the syllabus text. The summary is ours (not ISTQB text).
> For the exact content, see the section and page given below in the official PDF, or the file in `syllabus-text/`.

## Summary (ours)

The plan records the means and schedule, serves as communication and shows alignment with policy and strategy. Typical content: context and scope, assumptions and constraints, stakeholders, communication, risk register, test approach (levels, types, techniques, criteria, metrics, data, environment) and budget and schedule. Reference: ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119-3.
