## Summary (ours)

Identifies risks (brainstorming, workshops, interviews, cause-effect diagrams) and assesses them (category, likelihood, impact, level and priority), quantitatively (likelihood × impact) or qualitatively (risk matrix). The results set the scope, levels, types, techniques, coverage, effort and priority of testing.
