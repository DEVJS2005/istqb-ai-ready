## Summary (ours)

Possible responses are mitigating through testing, accepting, transferring or having a contingency plan. To mitigate through testing: pick suitable testers, adjust independence, run reviews and static analysis, choose fitting techniques and coverage, target the affected characteristics and test dynamically including regression. Monitoring confirms the effect and spots new risks.
