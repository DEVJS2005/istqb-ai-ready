# 5.5. Defect Management

## Summary (ours)

The process takes each anomaly from logging to closure: log, analyse, classify, decide and close. An anomaly may turn out not to be a defect (false positive, change request). The report should allow resolution, quality tracking and process improvement. Typical fields: ID, title, date and author, test object and environment, context, steps to reproduce, expected and actual result, severity, priority, status and references.
