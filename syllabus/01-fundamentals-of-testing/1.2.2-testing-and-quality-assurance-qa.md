# 1.2.2. Testing and Quality Assurance (QA)

## Summary (ours)

Testing is product-oriented and corrective; quality assurance (QA) is process-oriented and preventive, and everyone shares responsibility for it. Test results serve testing (to fix defects) and QA (as feedback on the processes).
