## Summary (ours)

Lists the usual goals: evaluate work products, trigger failures and find defects, reach coverage, lower risk, check requirements and compliance, inform decisions, build confidence and validate. Which goals apply depends on the product, the test level, risks, the lifecycle and the business situation.
