# 1.5.3. Independence of Testing

## Summary (ours)

Ranges from none (the author) to very high (outside the organisation); mixing several levels usually works best. Independence brings different biases and viewpoints, but can isolate testers, create friction, weaken developers' sense of ownership and turn testing into a bottleneck.
