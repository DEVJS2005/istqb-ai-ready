## Summary (ours)

The conceptual base for everything else. Defines testing and its objectives, separates it from debugging and from quality assurance, introduces the error → defect → failure chain, the seven principles, the test process activities and their work products (testware), traceability, roles and tester skills.

**Chapter study time (syllabus):** 180 minutes.
