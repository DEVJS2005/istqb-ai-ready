# 1.5.2. Whole Team Approach

> **Shell.** This file does not contain the syllabus text. The summary is ours (not ISTQB text).

## Summary (ours)

A practice from XP in which any capable member can take any task and quality is everyone's job. It improves communication and synergy, with testers working alongside business people and developers. It may not fit contexts that demand high independence, such as safety-critical systems.
