# 3.2.4. Review Types

## Summary (ours)

Formality depends on the SDLC, maturity, criticality, legal requirements and the need for an audit trail. Informal reviews follow no defined process; walkthroughs are led by the author; technical reviews aim for technical consensus and are moderated; inspections are the most formal, collect metrics and do not allow the author to be leader or scribe.
