# 3.2.3. Roles and Responsibilities in Reviews

> **Shell.** This file does not contain the syllabus text. The summary is ours (not ISTQB text).

## Summary (ours)

The manager decides what to review and supplies resources; the author creates and fixes; the moderator (facilitator) runs the meetings; the scribe records anomalies and decisions; reviewers review; and the review leader owns the review as a whole.
